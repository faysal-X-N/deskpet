DeskPet is a lightweight, fully open-source desktop pet that lives on your screen as a floating overlay.

FEATURES
• Floating companion — the pet stays on top of all your apps, in portrait and landscape
• Touch interaction — tap to wave, drag to move, pinch to resize
• 9 standard animations (idle / running / jumping / waving and more), compatible with the Codex Pet standard and 2500+ community pet packs
• Autonomous behavior — when left alone, the pet walks around, jumps and looks around on its own
• Independent memory — every pet remembers its own screen position and size
• Survives task swiping — and if the system kills it, reopening the app brings the pet back automatically
• Import & backup — import community pet packs or GIFs, export/restore all pets for device migration
• Landscape-ready — a vertical side dock adapts to screen notches and gesture areas
• Power-save aware — automatically lowers frame rate in system power-save mode

PRIVACY
No INTERNET permission at all. The app runs 100% locally and collects nothing.

OPEN SOURCE
