Codex Pet v2 support and animation timing fix:
- Support Codex Pet v2 sheets (11 rows, two extra "look" rows); the nine
  standard animations keep working as before
- Row count is now derived from the sheet height (v1 = 9, v2 = 11) instead
  of being hard-coded to 9
- Fixed: v2 sheets played misaligned, leaking the next row into the frame
- Fixed: thumbnails for v2 sheets were cropped with the wrong row height
- Animation timing now follows the spec's per-frame durations
  (idle 3s -> 1.1s per cycle, running 1.6s -> 1.06s)
